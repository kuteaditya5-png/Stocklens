## New rules (research-only; tested against today's exported data)
- A rolling bullish continuation requires a completed green candle closing above `previous_six_high + buffer`, with a significant candle body and a close in the upper 35% of its range.
- A rolling bearish continuation requires a completed red candle closing below `previous_six_low - buffer`, with equivalent body/closing-location checks.
- A reversal requires that a previous confirmed rolling breakout or breakdown fail, with a later completed candle decisively reclaiming the previously broken level in the opposite direction.
- `buffer = max(2, 12% of the median high-low range of the previous six candles)` in **index points**; minimum candle body `max(5, 25% of the median six-bar range)`.
- Signals are spaced by at least three bars (15 minutes), except a genuine failed-break reversal can arrive after two. This reduces duplicate continuation signals.
- Entry/stop/T1/T2 index levels are **locked at the confirming five-minute candle close**. They are indicative references, not tradable index fills. Subsequent touch checks are observational, over six following five-minute bars, with stop-first precedence if stop and target touch in the same candle.

## Limitations and risk safeguards
- NSE/BSE cash-index **spot points are not CE/PE option premium points**. A +35 premium target has not been validated by this workbook. StockLens's CE/PE Option Advisor screens actual Upstox options separately and can return WAIT.
- The backtest page retains its original one-trade-per-day ORB simulation; it does *not* constitute a rolling-breakout or option-premium performance backtest.
- User screenshot and Yahoo export are comparable for chart interpretation but not independent market-feed validation.
- No option chain, liquidity, bid/ask quote timestamps, lot size or stop fills can be concluded from this file.
- The reference entry is at the confirmation candle's close; actual next available execution may differ, including gap/slippage. No orders are placed.
- This v1.7 addition does not run in the background or guarantee another breakout before the session ends. The web dashboard must remain open for in-browser alerts; broker token must be valid.
